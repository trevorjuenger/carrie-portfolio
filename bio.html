Biography

Photography, collage, video, and zine work

Carrie Juenger is a multidisciplinary conceptual artist working out of Southern Illinois. Her work embodies loneliness, loss, death, trauma, sex and the body.

The photography that she produces is both deeply personal and emotionally charged, using her form to communicate complex concepts and feelings. This work explores accessible themes such as how one feels in their skin, the death of a loved one, partnership and psychic wounds.

Her video work utilizes animal symbolism and voiceover of original writing to convey trauma — specifically loss of virginity, childhood divorce and marital turmoil. These short films have been featured in regional film festivals, including one exhibition in Belarus (Eastern Europe).

Carrie’s work can be found in multiple issues of local art and poetry zines. She promotes local artists by creating flyers by hand and has been featured at The Small Press Expo in St. Louis, Missouri. She continues her work with her newest zine titled ‘Fruits of Luncacy’.
